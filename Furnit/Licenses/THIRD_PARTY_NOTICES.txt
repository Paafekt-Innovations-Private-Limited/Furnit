Model format modifications
- Depth Anything V2 Metric Indoor Small: Paafekt converted the upstream Small metric
  indoor checkpoint to Core ML for iOS and ONNX for Android on-device inference.
- GeoCalib: Paafekt converted the upstream pinhole CNN weights to Core ML for iOS and
  ONNX for Android; the applications implement the remaining calibration optimization.
- RTMDet-Ins-m: Paafekt converted the OpenMMLab checkpoint/export to an FP16
  LiteRT/TFLite graph used by both iOS and Android. The export includes graph
  adaptations for mobile conversion and the apps' documented input/output contract.
